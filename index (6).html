Our Philosophy

Fire is the
Oldest Chef

At Ember, we trace the lineage of cooking back to its origins — the primal drama of flame, smoke, and heat. Our kitchen is centred around a custom wood-fired hearth using only native hardwoods sourced from sustainable forests.

Our menu evolves with the seasons, built on relationships with farmers within a 200km radius. Every dish tells a story of soil, climate, and craft — elevated by classical technique and daring creativity.

"Cooking is the art of transformation.
We transform ingredients into memories."
